## Software Project Stakeholders ##

1. Stakeholder: Students  
Role: Use the website to communicate with professors, check their availability, and schedule meetings.
Interest/Expectations: Want a simple and convenient way to communicate with professors and check their availability.
Influence on the System: Their feedback at the end of the semester can help improve the website’s features and usability.
How the System Affects the Stakeholder: Makes communication more organized, helps students plan their Senior Design projects more effectively, and provides an easier way to view professor availability.

2. Stakeholder: Professor  
Role: Use the website to communicate with students, manage their availability, and schedule meetings.
Interest/Expectations: Want a simple and organized way to communicate with students and reduce the number of students waiting outside the offices.
Influence on the System: Can provide feedback and suggest features based on their needs and experience using the system. 
How the System Affects the Stakeholder: Helps their professors manage their schedules more effectively, organize meetings with students, and reduce unnecessary office visits.

3. Stakeholder: Senior Design Coordinator  
Role: Oversees the advisor selection process, ensures that student groups are assigned advisors, and manages communication between students and professors when necessary.
Interest/Expectations: Wants a clear and organized overview of advisor availability, student groups, and the overall advisor selection process.
Influence on the System: Can manage and update advisor information, monitor team capacities, and make changes to ensure the advisor selection process runs smoothly.
How the System Affects the Stakeholder: Makes it easier to monitor the advisor selection process, identify groups that still need advisors, and ensure that scheduling and advisor assignments are handled efficiently.
