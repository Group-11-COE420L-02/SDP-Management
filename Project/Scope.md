## Scope ##

Develop a web-based management system to simplify and organize the senior design advisor selection process for three main types of users: students, professors, and the Senior Design Coordinator. The system will make it easier for students to find and choose suitable advisors for their projects by allowing them to filter professors from different CEN departments based on their areas of expertise.

After logging in, students will be able to view professor profiles, check their availability, and see how many teams they are currently supervising. The system will also send email reminders to notify students when they should begin searching for a senior design advisor. After selecting a professor, students can either schedule a meeting based on the professor’s available timeslots or send a message by email containing their project idea and team members. Students will also have a list of professors they have contacted to help them keep track of their advisor selection process.

Professors will have their own dashboard where they can communicate with students, manage their availability, and view the teams and meetings they have already scheduled. The Senior Design Coordinator will oversee the overall advisor selection process, manage student and professor information, monitor advisor team capacities, and ensure that the advisor selection process is progressing appropriately.

The system will focus on providing the core functionality required for the advisor selection process. Features outside the current project scope include displaying professors' previous projects or work on a public landing page, integration with university systems such as Banner or Blackboard, and an integrated chat system for ongoing communication between students and professors. Communication will instead be handled through email.

Overall, the system aims to make the advisor selection process more organized, transparent, and convenient for students, professors, and the Senior Design Coordinator.

### Major Deliverables: ###

- A functional web-based Senior Design Advisor Management System.
- A student interface for searching and filtering professors, viewing profiles and availability, submitting project information, requesting meetings, and tracking contacted professors.
- A professor dashboard for managing availability, viewing teams and meetings, and communicating with students.
- A Senior Design Coordinator dashboard for managing users, professor information, advisor capacities, and overseeing the advisor selection process.
- A database for storing user accounts, professor information, teams, project ideas, meeting schedules, and advisor requests.
- An email notification system for advisor-search reminders and relevant updates.
- System requirements and design documentation.
- Testing documentation and results.
- A final project report and project demonstration.
