## Project Risks ##

1. Risk ID: R1  
   Risk Description: Professor availability information may be inaccurate  
   Possible Cause: Professors can even forget to update how many team slots they actually have available.  
   Probability: Medium  
   Impact: High  
   Mitigation/Response Strategy: Allow professors to easily update their availability and send reminders to keep their status updated  

2. Risk ID: R2  
   Risk Description: Meeting scheduling conflicts can happen  
   Possible Cause: Several students may try to book the same time slot, or a professor's availability may change  
   Probability: Medium  
   Impact: Medium  
   Mitigation/Response Strategy: Instantly remove booked time slots and allow professors to update or cancel their available times  

3. Risk ID: R3  
   Risk Description: Requirements might change or be unclear during development  
   Possible Cause: Students and professors may ask for new features or changes after the development phase has started  
   Probability: Medium  
   Impact: High  
   Mitigation/Response Strategy: Confirm the main requirements early and regularly talk with users before making major changes  

4. Risk ID: R4  
   Risk Description: Technical difficulties may occur when integrating website features  
   Possible Cause: Features like chat, notifications, scheduling, and professor dashboards may be difficult to integrate correctly  
   Probability: Medium  
   Impact: High  
   Mitigation/Response Strategy: Develop and test features separately before integrating them, and perform regular testing throughout development  

5. Risk ID: R5  
   Risk Description: Security and privacy issues may occur  
   Possible Cause: Unauthorized users might possibly gain access to student project ideas, private messages, meetings, or professor information  
   Probability: Low  
   Impact: High  
   Mitigation/Response Strategy: Should add a secure university login, use role-based access for students and professors, and securely protect stored user data
