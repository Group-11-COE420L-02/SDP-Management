# Functional Requirements #

## Mahmoud Contributions ## 
### FR-01 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall allow students to search for, and filter professors based on their areas of expertise.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-01/Student  
Contributor: Mahmoud  

### FR-02 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall allow students to view a professor's available meeting timeslots and submit a meeting request for an available timeslot.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-01/Student  
Contributor: Mahmoud  

### FR-03 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall display each professor's areas of expertise and current number of supervised teams.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-01/Student  
Contributor: Mahmoud  

### FR-04 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall allow students to submit their project ideas and team member information when contacting a potential advisor.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-01/Student  
Contributor: Mahmoud  

### FR-05 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall maintain a list of professors contacted by each student and display the status of each contact or meeting request.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-01/Student  
Contributor: Mahmoud  

## Deema Contributions ##
### FR-06 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall allow Senior Design students and professors to login in using their university emails only.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-02/Student/Professor  
Contributor: Deema  

### FR-07 ###
Functional Requirement: The system allows students and professors to send and receive messages through the website.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-02/Student/Professor  
Contributor: Deema  

### FR-08 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall notify and professor when they receive a message or meeting update.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-02/Student/Professor  
Contributor: Deema  

### FR-09 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall allow students and professors to cancel or request to reschedule an upcoming meeting.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-02/Student/Professor  
Contributor: Deema  

### FR-10 ###
Functional Requirement: The system allows students and professors to view their upcoming meetings with professors.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-02/Student/Professor  
Contributor: Deema  

## Khaled Contributions ##
### FR-11 ###
Functional Requirement: The system should allow professors to accept a student group as one of their Senior Design teams.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-03/Professor  
Contributor: Khaled  

### FR-12 ###
Functional Requirement: The system is supposed to allow the professors to view the project idea and team member information of a student group before accepting them.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-03/Professor  
Contributor: Khaled  

### FR-13 ###
Functional Requirement: The system prevents a professor from accepting more than 4 Senior Design teams.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-03/Professor  
Contributor: Khaled  

### FR-14 ###
Functional Requirement: The system updates the professor's remaining team capacity after a student group is accepted.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-03/Professor  
Contributor: Khaled  

### FR-15 ###
Functional Requirement: The system notifies the student group when a professor accepts them as a Senior Design team.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-03/Professor  
Contributor: Khaled  

## Karim Contributions ##
### FR-16 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall allow the Senior Design Coordinator to view a list of all registered Senior Design teams and their assigned advisors.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-04/Coordinator  
Contributor: Karim  

### FR-17 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall display each team’s project title, team members, assigned advisor, and confirmation status to the Senior Design Coordinator.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-04/Coordinator  
Contributor: Karim  

### FR-18 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall allow the Senior Design Coordinator to confirm a team and its advisor’s assignment after reviewing their information.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-04/Coordinator  
Contributor: Karim  

### FR-19 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall allow the Senior Design Coordinator to reject an invalid or incomplete team assignment and provide a reason for the rejection.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-04/Coordinator  
Contributor: Karim  

### FR-20 ###
Functional Requirement: The system shall notify the relevant students and professors when the Senior Design Coordinator confirms or rejects their team assignment.  
Source Scenario/Stakeholder: S-04/Coordinator  
Contributor: Karim
